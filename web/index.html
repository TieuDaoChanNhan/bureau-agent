Your association's busywork, investigated by an agent.
Every decision stays yours.

Boards run events on top of their studies: registrations, fees, teams, rooms, trips and dozens of messages. Bureau Agent finds what needs attention, investigates it with tools, and proposes one action with its evidence. Nothing is sent, changed or booked until an organizer approves it.
