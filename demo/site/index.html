Agentic operations · for volunteer-run associations

Your association's busywork, investigated by an agent.

Boards run events on top of their studies: registrations, fees, teams, rooms, trips and dozens of messages. Bureau Agent finds what needs attention, investigates it with tools, and proposes one action with its evidence. Review the evidence, edit the reply, and approve the next step. This demo applies your decisions only to fictional data in your browser.

The problem

Student boards run real operations with no operations team.

Scattered records

Registrations in a form, fees in a bank export, teams in a spreadsheet, questions in the inbox. Nobody sees the whole event.

Mistakes found too late

An unpaid fee, a member in two teams or a missing room shows up on the day, when fixing it costs the most.

Every answer needs a lookup

"Can my friend from another school join?" means reading the rules and the records before replying, dozens of times a week.

Why you can trust it

The AI investigates. Code enforces the rules. People decide.

✓Nothing happens without approval

The agent only proposes. Every data change, reply and payment link waits for an organizer, and each approval is logged.

§Rules checked in code

Eligibility, team sizes and budgets are verified by code, not by the model. Below a 0.70 identity match the agent must ask instead of acting.

iTransparent by default

Each proposal shows the tools used and the evidence found. Drafted replies say they were written with AI assistance (EU AI Act, Art. 50).
