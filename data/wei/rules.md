# WEI 2026 — DEMO organizer briefing and policies

## §1 Source and status
WEI means weekend d'intégration. The user briefing identifies the organizer as the kès, the student association of École polytechnique: it collects student registrations, checks payments, rents coaches and searches for a resort-like venue with lodging, cooking facilities and space for group activities. Meals are included in the student participation fee. The kès buys groceries and transports them to the venue. This describes the intended workflow, not an official kès policy document.

All participant identities, contact addresses, payments, messages and operating policies in this dataset are fictional DEMO records. At the user's request, the demo uses 100 students, 9–11 October 2026 and a maximum of €120 per person for round-trip coaches, two nights of lodging, groceries and their transport. These numbers and the policies below have NOT been confirmed by the kès. No venue, coach, quote or booking is confirmed.

## §2 Registration and payment checks
The demo registration list contains 100 students. Submitting the registration form and paying the participation fee are separate steps. The kès checks registrations against payment records before confirming an individual's participation; the final trip also requires organizer validation of the logistics plan. A registration alone is not proof of payment, and a payment alone is not a confirmed coach or room reservation.

In this fixture, 96 payments match registered email addresses exactly. Nathan Moreau (w06), Sarah Lefebvre (w07) and Hugo Garnier (w08) have no payment record. The transfer b90 from C. An organizer must review the evidence before linking it; a participant's message is not by itself verification. Use the registered contact address when asking for a payment reference or receipt. Do not request bank passwords or full bank account details.

## §3 Provisional fee, budget and meals
The recorded demo participation payment is €112 (11200 cents) per student, including round-trip coaches, two nights of lodging, meals and grocery transport. The illustrative allocation matching option A is €35 for coaches, €55 for lodging, €20 for groceries and €2 for food transport per person. These amounts are reassigned fictional allocations within the unchanged total, not supplier quotes or the final approved price, and await confirmation by the kès.

The separate €120 (12000 cents) per-person ceiling is the maximum planning budget for the full package, including meals and food transport. It is not an additional charge or the amount to request by default. Any difference between the provisional fee and the validated trip cost requires an explicit organizer decision before communicating an adjusted fee.

The kès buys the groceries and transports them to the venue. The demo food allocation is €2000 for groceries and €200 for their transport for 100 students, already included in the participation fee and package totals. Do not request a separate meal surcharge. Organizers must confirm the menu, quantities, dietary needs, purchasing arrangements and food transport capacity, storage and handling. The transport method is undecided. For this DEMO only, payment is due by 1 October 2026 at 23:59 Europe/Paris, by bank transfer with reference "WEI" and the student's full name. Payment instructions must come from the organizers; this dataset contains no bank details.

## §4 Refunds — DEMO policy
No refund after 1 October, except exceptional cases decided by the organizers. Requests before 1 October are refunded within 7 days. This is an illustrative policy inherited from the sample, not an official kès commitment; borderline dates and exceptional cases must be referred to the organizers. Verify the registration, payment and request date, then prepare a reply or referral for organizer review. Do not issue a refund or promise an exception automatically.

## §5 Dates, rented coaches and meeting points
The unconfirmed demo trip runs from Friday 9 October to Sunday 11 October 2026, with two nights of lodging. The intended transport is two rented coaches with 53 passenger seats each (106 seats total for 100 students), departing after classes from the École polytechnique campus in Palaiseau and returning to the same campus on Sunday afternoon. Both coaches and their seating capacities are demo assumptions awaiting verification. All trip dates and times use Europe/Paris (UTC+02:00 for these dates).

Friday departure after 17:00, arrival before 21:00 and no overnight travel are demo planning constraints. Exact pickup and drop-off points, headcount, luggage capacity, coach boarding accessibility and both journey times require organizer and operator confirmation. Send a concrete meeting point or return schedule only after a plan is validated. Do not present a rail itinerary as a rented coach booking.

## §6 Venue, shared kitchen and group activities
Trouville-Deauville is an assumed search area inherited from the demo, not a selected WEI destination. No particular resort or property has been chosen. Search for a venue suitable for 100 students over two nights, with lodging, a kitchen that the group is allowed to use, and suitable shared indoor and outdoor activity spaces.

Before selecting a venue, the organizers must confirm bed capacity, availability, kitchen equipment and permission for group cooking, any kitchen or activity-space charges, event and noise rules, permitted activity hours, and access between the coach drop-off and the shared spaces. A generic lodging listing or a kitchen label does not establish these permissions. The shared meal plan and the demo food allocation included in the total still require confirmation, including suitable storage and handling for groceries brought by the kès.

## §7 Rooms and accessibility
Inès Roux (w03) and Emma Laurent (w05) have declared step-free needs in their demo registrations. Plan for at least two step-free rooms and obtain organizer verification of actual access to bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen and activity areas, plus suitable coach boarding arrangements. The declared needs are not evidence that a supplier's facilities have been checked.

The organizers assign rooms only after the venue and its room details are validated. Room wishes may be collected in the registration form, but do not promise a room assignment, suitable bathroom, shared-space accessibility or transport assistance before those checks are complete.

## §8 Jinko sourcing and organizer decisions
Jinko hotel search supports live calls and replay of cached responses. The demo scales a cached price for one double room to 50 rooms for 100 students; this is not a confirmed group quote or availability check. Jinko ground search returned 404 for the current key, so the two-coach transport options remain illustrative. Composition includes the demo grocery and food-transport budgets once in the full package price. If hotel search is unavailable, the planner uses illustrative fallback packages, including option A at €112 per person. Those packages are not Jinko quotes. Private coach hire, current supplier prices, the group room block and venue permissions must be checked by the organizers before a plan is treated as operational.

Prepare options and questions for organizer review. No booking, payment, supplier contact, participant message or room assignment is executed merely because a demo plan is feasible. Outstanding kitchen permissions, group activity rules, included food budget, grocery purchasing and transport arrangements, accessibility and return-coach details must stay visible until confirmed.

## §9 What to bring — provisional advice
The demo packing suggestion is an identity card and warm clothes. Bedding or a sleeping bag, cooking equipment and activity supplies depend on the selected venue and must be confirmed by the organizers before sending a final packing list.
