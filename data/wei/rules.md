# WEI 2026 information (sample)

## Dates
Friday 9 October (departure from Paris after class) to Sunday 11 October (return in the afternoon).

## Price and payment
The price covers travel and lodging only; meals are paid on site. Payment is due by 1 October, by bank transfer with the reference "WEI" and your full name.

## Refunds
No refund after 1 October, except exceptional cases decided by the organizers. Requests before 1 October are refunded within 7 days.

## Rooms
Rooms are assigned by the organizers. Room wishes can be given in the registration form. Participants with accessibility needs are placed in step-free rooms.

## What to bring
ID card, sleeping bag, warm clothes.
